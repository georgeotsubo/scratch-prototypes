Icons .pl-icon · Figma page "iOS icons"

Injected as an inline SVG sprite by icons.js — external <use href="file.svg#id"> is blocked on file://, and these prototypes get opened directly as often as they get served. Monochrome icons are currentColor, so they take the colour of whatever they sit in. The three stars and map-pin are vector paths that are two-tone by design, painting fixed colours (icon/rating / icon/disabled / icon/red + white) directly instead of currentColor.
